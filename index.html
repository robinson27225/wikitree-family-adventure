Family Adventure

Turn a child's family story into a coloring and activity workbook. Everything stays in this browser.

Imports the public ancestors from WikiTree. WikiTree lists two biological parents, so add step-parents, guardians and others by hand afterwards.

Parents & guardians

Add as many as the child has, in any roles. Then add each person's parents, and so on.

To make a PDF, choose “Save as PDF” in the print window.

Tap a color, then tap any shape to color it on screen. Print it to color on paper.
