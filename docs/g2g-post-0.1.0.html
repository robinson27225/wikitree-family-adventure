Title
Family Adventure: a free app that turns your family tree into a coloring and activity workbook for children
Tags
wt_apps

Do the children in your family know where they come from? I made Family Adventure to help get them interested. It turns a child's family tree into a printable workbook of coloring pages, tracing sheets and activities, and the fan chart of their ancestors can be colored on screen too.

Open Family Adventure · Space page with screenshots and instructions

How it works

  1. Type the child's name and choose an age range.
  2. Enter a WikiTree ID for the child or one of their parents and press Import, or type the family in by hand.
  3. Color on screen, then print the workbook (or save it as a PDF).

What is in it

  • A fan chart of the child's ancestors, with their name in the middle.
  • A card for each person to draw, a family journey through the places they came from, and drawings to color.
  • Ages 3 to 5: names as dashed lines to trace, and an alphabet tracing sheet. Parents and grandparents.
  • Ages 6 to 8 and 9 to 12: more generations, patterns, interview questions and a timeline.
  • Ages 13 and older: research pages instead of a certificate: place research, an interview log, a research log and "What I learned".

Any family shape works. You can add as many parents and guardians as a child has, in any roles, with no limit of two and no gender fields.

A few things to know

  • WikiTree shows two biological parents, so add step-parents, guardians and others by hand after importing.
  • WikiTree hides birthplaces and dates for living people, so you will need to type those in. The older pages leave a blank place for them, which makes a good interview question.
  • Everything you enter stays in your own browser. The only thing sent anywhere is the request to WikiTree when you press Import.
